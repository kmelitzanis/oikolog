Tour

A look inside the app.

lockoikolog.home/bills
Features

Everything a home needs so nothing slips through.

From the power bill to the car service: every obligation with a date, an amount and a history.

receipt_long

Bills & payments

Monthly, quarterly or one-off, with the next due date worked out for you. Partial payments, and loans where you see how much is left to repay.

savings

Envelopes & savings

Set how much you spend each pay cycle. Whatever is left over goes to savings in one click.

payments

Income

Salary and other income are marked when the money actually lands, so you see how early or late it came.

mark_email_read

Invoices from email Beta

Connect Gmail or Outlook and Oikolog reads providers’ invoices to suggest the amount. It never sends or deletes anything.

family_restroom

For the whole family

Shared bills, an invite code, and a push notification when someone pays.

qr_code_scanner

Shopping lists

Lists with quantities, a product catalogue and barcode scanning with your phone camera, with nutrition data.

restaurant_menu

Recipes & meal plan

Import recipes from a URL and plan the week; the ingredients go straight to a shopping list.

directions_car

Vehicles

Mileage, service reminders, a work history, cost per kilometre and OBD diagnostics over Bluetooth.

For the whole family

One home, shared by everyone.

Create your family and send your people an invite code. Once they join, you share the household bills, see who paid what, and nobody has to ask “did you pay it?” again.

  • group_addInvite code, owner and member roles, ownership transfer
  • sync_altShared bills, income, bank accounts and vehicles. Whatever you don’t share stays yours alone.
  • notifications_activeA phone notification when someone pays, and who paid what
  • historyAn activity feed: who added or changed what

Recipes

Paste a link. The recipe writes itself.

Give it a recipe link and Oikolog takes the name, photo, servings, times, ingredients and steps from the page, split into sections just like the original.

  • linkWorks with most big recipe sites
  • starDedicated support for akispetretzikis.com
  • straightenGreek cooking units are recognised and normalised
  • calendar_view_weekA weekly meal plan, with the week’s ingredients sent to the shopping list in one click

On your phone · PWA

Like an app, without an app store.

Oikolog is a Progressive Web App (PWA): add it to your home screen straight from the browser and it opens full screen like a regular app, with notifications and a bottom navigation bar. No app store, and no updates to wait for.

  • check_circleA notification when someone in the family pays
  • check_circleBarcode scanning with the camera for the shopping list
  • check_circleOBD diagnostics for your car over Bluetooth, right from the browser
Self-hosted

Your data, in your own home.

Oikolog runs on your own server, NAS or VPS. Your bills and payments never pass through a third party.

  • check_circleOne docker compose up and you are ready
  • check_circleTwo-factor authentication (TOTP)
  • check_circleMail passwords are stored encrypted, or not needed at all with Microsoft sign-in

Install

Up and running in minutes.

All you need is Docker. The app comes ready inside the image, and MySQL comes with the compose file.

For a NAS, HTTPS and notifications, see the Docker guide.

Get the project

git clone https://github.com/kmelitzanis/oikolog.git cd oikolog

Set your details

cp .env.example .env docker run --rm kostasmel/oikolog-app php artisan key:generate --show # put the key in APP_KEY, along with APP_URL and database passwords

Start it

docker compose up -d docker compose exec app php artisan make:user --admin

Open http://localhost:8000 and sign in.
